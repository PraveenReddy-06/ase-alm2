About the prototype

How CivicFlow works

A simple end-to-end concept for making community water and drainage problems easier to report and follow.

Problem being addressed

Drainage blockages, overflowing drains, water shortages, leaks and possible water-quality problems can go unreported or be difficult to follow up when citizens have no common reporting channel. Unclear locations, missing evidence and limited status visibility can slow prioritization.

Proposed approach

CivicFlow gives citizens a structured form to describe an issue, add a location, attach an optional photo and select its impact level. A tracker displays each report's status. A simulated authority view provides a priority queue and controls for changing status.

Workflow

1
Report

Issue type, impact level, locality, description and optional photo.

2
Record

The prototype validates basic inputs and saves the report in local browser storage.

3
Review

Authority mode sorts and highlights higher-priority reports.

4
Track

Status progresses from Submitted to Acknowledged, In Progress and Resolved.

Important limitations

  • All records stay in the current browser and device; they are not shared with other users.
  • The authority view is a demonstration, not a secure authenticated staff portal.
  • Photos may be stored locally and will not be uploaded to a service.
  • Coordinates depend on user permission and browser/device support.
  • This application is not monitored by a municipality and must not be used for emergencies.
